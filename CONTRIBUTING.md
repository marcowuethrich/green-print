# Contributing

GreenPrint is a personal portfolio and template repository, so it does not take outside
contributions: pull requests and issues are limited to collaborators. You are welcome to fork it,
copy the agentic setup into your own project (`docs/agentic/README.md`, *Use it in your project*)
and adapt it under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

How changes are made here: [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) and
[`.claude/skills/feature-workflow/SKILL.md`](.claude/skills/feature-workflow/SKILL.md).
